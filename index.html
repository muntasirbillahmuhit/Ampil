Ampil Guide & Setup

📱 Floating Controls on Android

  • Single Tap Bubble: Open mini mixer with ± volume & mute.
  • Long Press Bubble: Trigger full system volume slider.
  • Drag Bubble: Move anywhere with smooth edge snapping.

⚠️ Android 13+ Setup Note

If the Accessibility toggle is grayed out on Android 13+: Open App Info → tap the 3 dots in the top right → "Allow restricted settings" → verify device PIN → toggle Ampil ON.

🔊 Web Synthesizer Tone

Real-time multi-frequency sine wave tones give instant volume feedback for Media (440Hz), Ring (587Hz), Alarm (880Hz), and Notification (659Hz).
